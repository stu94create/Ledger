Where is your logo or photo?
image
A direct link to the picture itself, ending in .png or .jpg.
How to get a web address for a picture on your phone
  1. In Safari, go to postimages.org. No account is needed.
  2. Tap Choose images, then pick your logo from Photos or Files.
  3. When it finishes, find the box labelled Direct link and copy it.
  4. Paste it into the box above.

Links from iCloud Drive, Google Drive, Dropbox or OneDrive won't work here. They open a web page rather than the picture itself.

Contact card (vCard)

A contact card is a small file people can open to add you to their Contacts in one tap. It uses the same details as your signature, so there's nothing extra to type.

Optional. If you have saved your card and put the .vcf file online, paste its address here and the signature gains an "Add me to your contacts" link. Anything at a web address is public. Only link a card that holds details you'd put on a poster, and never a home address.

05Copy your signature

How to add it to your email app

Mail on iPhone and iPad

  1. Press Copy signature.
  2. Go to Settings, Apps, Mail, Signature, and paste.
  3. If the colours and layout disappear, shake the phone (or use the undo gesture) and choose Undo Change Attributes.

Outlook (Windows or Mac)

  1. Press Copy signature.
  2. Go to Settings, Mail, Signatures, then New signature.
  3. Paste into the signature box and save.

Gmail on the web

  1. Press Copy signature.
  2. Open Settings, See all settings, scroll to Signature and choose Create new.
  3. Paste, then press Save Changes at the bottom.

Apple Mail on Mac

  1. Press Copy signature.
  2. Open Mail, Settings, Signatures, choose the account and press the plus button.
  3. Paste into the right-hand box. Untick "Always match my default message font".

On a shared or borrowed device

Sign Off remembers what you type on this device so you can come back to it. If the device isn't yours, open the menu at the top right and choose "Clear everything and start again" when you're finished.
